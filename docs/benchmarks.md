int8 is 4.0x smaller than float32.

Honest note: on the JVM the int8 path is usually *not* faster — integer math
without SIMD plus per-call input quantization costs more than it saves at this
model size. The int8 win here is the smaller model (APK size, memory, download)
and suitability for phone DSP/NPU kernels, where the integer path is what runs fast.
